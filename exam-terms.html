📜 Terms & Conditions Before Examination

  • 👤
    Registered Account Requirement: Students must use their own registered college account. Impersonation will result in immediate disqualification.
  • 🔒
    Single Attempt Policy: Only one attempt is permitted per examination. Submitted or terminated examinations cannot be retaken.
  • 🚫
    Prohibition of Collusion: Students must not share answers or communicate with others during the examination.
  • 🌐
    Unauthorized Resources: Students must not use external websites, search engines, AI tools, notes, or unauthorized materials.
  • 🔄
    Session Integrity: Students must not refresh, close, or attempt to manipulate the examination browser session.
  • 🖥
    Full-Screen Requirement: Students must remain in full-screen mode throughout the entire duration of the examination.
  • ⚠️
    Exit Violation Monitoring: Leaving full-screen mode will trigger warnings. Pressing Escape or switching windows counts as a violation.
  • 🛑
    Three-Strike Termination Rule: Three full-screen exit violations will result in automatic examination termination with zero marks awarded under institutional malpractice policy.
  • ⏱
    Automatic Time Expiry: The examination will automatically submit when the allotted 60-minute duration expires. Unsaved answers will not be counted.
  • ⚖️
    Authoritative Disciplinary Decision: The examination authority's decision regarding malpractice, violations, and score adjustments is final.

📘 Examination Rules and Regulations

  • 1.
    Read each question carefully before choosing your answer.
  • 2.
    Select the single most appropriate answer from options A, B, C, or D.
  • 3.
    Students may navigate freely between all 50 questions using the question palette while the exam is active.
  • 4.
    Unanswered questions receive zero (0) marks.
  • 5.
    Correct answers receive one (1) mark each.
  • 6.
    Incorrect answers receive zero (0) marks (no negative marking).
  • 7.
    Do not leave full-screen mode at any point during testing.
  • 8.
    The examination cannot be restarted after submission or termination.
  • 9.
    The authoritative server clock strictly controls the official examination deadline. Late starters receive only the remaining duration.
